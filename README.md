### About the Experiment 

The median tissue culture infectious dose (TCID₅₀) is the dilution of a virus required to infect 50% of a given cell culture. In this assay, a susceptible cell line is infected with serial dilutions of a virus sample. After infection, the cells are examined microscopically for virus-induced morphological changes such as cell rounding, detachment, syncytia formation, or cell lysis, collectively known as cytopathic effects (CPE). The dilution at which 50% of the wells show CPE is calculated using methods such as the Reed-Muench or Spearman–Kärber methods. The final result is expressed as TCID₅₀ per millilitre (TCID₅₀/mL).
